29. 다음 글의 밑줄 친 부분 중, 어법상 틀린 것은? [3점]
Managers who listen well are often called “nice,” as though empathy were a pleasant extra rather than a professional skill. Research on retention tells a different story. People rarely leave an organization over pay alone; they leave when they feel that no one in the organization ③understanding what their work costs them. A leader who asks questions and ①acts on the answers signals that a problem is ②worth solving, and that signal is what keeps talented people from quietly looking elsewhere. Empathy is not the same as agreement. It is the discipline of seeing another person’s situation clearly ④to notice what they are up against, and teams ⑤that practise it systematically report lower turnover than teams that depend on individual talent. In short, what leaders pay attention to determines who stays.

* 밑줄은 border-bottom으로 렌더링되어 PDF/이미지 변환 시에도 소실되지 않습니다. ①~⑤ 번호는 ③ 같이 상단 배지로 표시.

정답 및 해설 보기 — 클릭하여 펼치기 (인쇄 시 2페이지로 분리 권장)

정답: ③

  • ① acts — 옳음. 선행사 A leader(단수) + 관계절 내 who asks ... and acts ... 병렬 동사 모두 3인칭 단수 -s 필요.
  • ② worth solving — 옳음. be worth + 동명사 구문. worth to solve가 틀린 형태.
  • ③ understanding → understands / is understood — 틀림. 주어는 no one(단수). 뒤에 완전한 절의 서술어가 와야 하는데 현재분사 understanding만으로는 절이 완성되지 않음. 분사구문으로 쓰려면 접속사(without understanding)나 주어+동사(no one understands)가 필요. 상태동사 understand의 진행형 is understanding도 어색.
  • ④ to notice — 옳음. The discipline of seeing ... clearly to notice ... 목적의 부정사. to noticing 불가.
  • ⑤ what leaders pay attention to determines — 옳음. what-절이 주어 → 단수 취급, determines 단수 동사 일치. pay attention to 관용구 + 전치사 잔류(stranded preposition) 허용.

원문 이미지 하단 잘림(“attention to determines who stays.”)으로 ⑤가 what-절 전체에 걸린 것으로 복원. “what leaders pay”만 밑줄이면 역시 주어-동사 일치는 옳음.
